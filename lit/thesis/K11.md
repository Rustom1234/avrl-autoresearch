# K11: Adversarial Prior-Art Check on H11

## Closest precedent verified

MechRL (Khadka, 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26343) trains a single RL policy that removes edges from GPT-2 small's computation graph, guided by a faithfulness reward. Trained across 12 behaviors, the frozen policy recovers a faithful circuit for each and transfers to unseen behaviors with no further per-behaviour search. Its own abstract states the policy "does not match a per-behaviour search on circuit size or cost." No cheap pre-measurement features as policy state, no operation across training checkpoints, only GPT-2 small. Matches H11's own characterization in its source 11.

## Two other close works verified

PAHQ (2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23264): mixed-precision, training-free speedup cutting ACDC's runtime up to 80% and memory up to 30%. It accelerates whatever ACDC already decided to probe; it does not choose what to probe next.

Certified Interventional Fidelity (2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08349): anytime-valid confidence sequences (Hoeffding-style, variance-adaptive betting) certifying whether a causal effect is real or a sampling artifact, cutting confirmation cost 10-30x versus fixed-sample intervals on GPT-2 circuits and MNIST abstractions. A stopping rule for an existing probe sequence, not a policy that decides where to probe.

Neither substitutes for an active, cross-axis (layer x feature x checkpoint) selection policy; both are compatible with, not competitors to, H11's core mechanism.

## Strongest "cannot work" argument

H11's own source 8, MIB (Mueller et al. 2025, ICML, https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13151), already reports SAE features are no better than raw neurons for causal-variable localization across 4 tasks and 5 models. H11 section 4 concedes this is "the shakiest of the three legs." That understates it: if cheap signals (activation norms, SAE activity, EAP scores) don't correlate with which components an expensive intervention confirms causal, there is nothing for a bandit or RL policy to exploit, and the triage premise collapses to random search plus training cost. Not a hypothetical risk; an existing negative result the thesis already cites against itself.

## Judgment

H11 gates on exactly this risk (kill criterion 1, first two weeks), so the flaw is anticipated, not fatal to the proposal outright. But the year rests on an unproven premise the closest adjacent benchmark (MIB) already found false in a narrower, one-shot setting. The surviving gap is narrow: whether sequential state (prior probe outcomes plus checkpoint-adjacency, combined with cheap scores) makes signals predictive where MIB's one-shot raw-SAE-activation ranking was not. MechRL never tested this, having no cheap-feature state; MIB never tested it, doing no sequential probing. That combination is unclaimed as of September 2026, UNVERIFIED beyond the sources checked in this five-minute pass.
