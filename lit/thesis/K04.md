# K04: Adversarial Prior-Art Kill Attempt on H04 (Active Human-Preference Collection)

## Three closest works

1. **"Random Is Hard to Beat: Active Selection in online DPO with Modern LLMs,"** Oh, Lee, Park, Yu, Bae, Noh, arXiv:2604.02766 (April 3, 2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02766
   Runs H04's own Phase-1 ablation: uncertainty-based active selection vs. random, online DPO, modern LLM backbones, reward-model and LLM-judge proxies, across harmlessness/helpfulness/instruction-following. Finding: uncertainty-based APL gives "negligible improvements in proxy win-rates compared to Random," and win-rate gains that do appear come with capability degradation active selection does not mitigate better than random.

2. **"ActiveUltraFeedback: Efficient Preference Data Generation using Active Learning,"** arXiv:2603.09692 (2026), lasgroup/ETH Zurich. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09692 (already cited in H04)
   Matches or beats full-data DPO/IPO/SimPO baselines at roughly 1/6 the labels via DRTS/DeltaUCB, 76.70% win rate vs. random/full-data. H04's own named baseline to beat.

3. **"Not All Preference Pairs Are Created Equal,"** Yang et al., EMNLP 2024 Findings, arXiv:2406.17312. https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.17312 (already cited in H04)
   Margin-based selection beats random/large-margin, and front-loading budget in early iterations beats even spreading, on real iterative preference learning, not simulation. Covers H04's "adapt as earlier labels arrive" claim.

## Strongest reason a committee says "already done / cannot work"

Work #1 is fatal-shaped: it is essentially H04's Phase-1 experiment, already run on modern LLMs in an online DPO loop, finding active selection (uncertainty-based) does not meaningfully beat random on win-rate, and surfacing a capability-collapse failure mode H04's current metrics do not track. A committee could argue the field has moved from "does active beat random" (now often "no" for uncertainty-only) to "does smarter acquisition beat random," which #2 and #3 already answer "yes" for single-round or offline-pool settings.

## Judgment

The area is crowded and now includes a negative result on H04's own first experiment. This does not kill H04 outright: its premise is that uncertainty-only is insufficient and a learned, clustering-aware, multi-round-adaptive selector is needed. arXiv:2604.02766 tested only static uncertainty selection, not a learned selector, not embedding clustering, and not round-over-round reward-model/policy co-drift. That specific combination, evaluated against DRTS/DeltaUCB (not just random) across 3-5 iterative DPO rounds with capability collapse tracked, is untested. The gap is real but narrower than H04's plan implies; Phase 1's kill criterion (beat DRTS/DeltaUCB) should be tightened to also require beating random with a capability-preserving margin, given arXiv:2604.02766.
