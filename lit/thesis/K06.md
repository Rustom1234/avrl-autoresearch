# K6: Kill attempt on H6 (Active Experiment Design for Scaling Laws)

## Two more close works found

1. **Power-Law Entropy Search (PLES)** — Chen, Ament, Eriksson, Balandat, Low, Bakshy, Lin, "Efficiently Estimating Optimal Hyperparameter Scaling Laws through Power-Law Entropy Search," arXiv:2609.01431 (Sept 2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01431
   Multi-fidelity Bayesian optimization with an uncertainty-reduction-per-cost acquisition function, applied directly to fitting optimal-hyperparameter scaling laws across model and data scale. Reports convergence to accurate scaling laws using "less than one-tenth of the computational budget required by conventional grid search and other baselines" — the same headline number and the same Phase-1 milestone H6 proposes to hit. Authors include Meta's Ax/BoTorch BO team (Ament, Eriksson, Balandat, Bakshy), i.e. a well-resourced group already shipping this exact tool. UNVERIFIED whether PLES covers LR+batch-size jointly or one axis at a time; abstract does not claim cross-architecture policy transfer.

2. **ADMIRE-BayesOpt** — Chen, Ouyang, Pearce, Hartvigsen, Schwarz, "ADMIRE-BayesOpt: Accelerated Data Mixture RE-weighting for LLMs with Bayesian Optimization," arXiv:2508.11551 (Aug 2025). https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.11551
   Frames data-mixture selection as black-box, multi-fidelity Bayesian optimization, tested 1M-7B params, releases 460 full runs (13,000+ GPU-hours), reports >500% speedup on the largest experiments and explores small-to-large transfer. This directly performs active/BO-based selection on the mixture axis, one of the four axes H6 section 3(d) calls "so far stayed largely separate."

Also relevant but not counted as one of the two: JF-HPO (arXiv:2606.03073, June 2026) already treats model size *and* training budget jointly as fidelity dimensions, further eroding the "joint-axis" novelty claim.

## Strongest reason a committee kills this

PLES (2609.01431) already delivers H6's Phase-1 success metric — active/BO-based scaling-law fitting at <10% of grid-search compute — from a team with deep applied-BO infrastructure (Meta Ax/BoTorch), published five months after SL². Meanwhile ADMIRE-BayesOpt independently active-optimizes the mixture axis with multi-fidelity and scale transfer, and JF-HPO independently jointly varies size+budget fidelity. Three well-funded groups shipped adjacent pieces of H6's "joint active design" claim inside a 13-month window (2508, 2604, 2606, 2609). A committee's objection: H6's Phase 1-2 plan (6 weeks to reproduce single-axis, months 2-6 to add mixture and test transfer) is racing against industry labs already iterating faster than an academic year allows, and the "joint" contribution risks being "stitch together three papers that already exist" rather than new science.

## Judgment

already_done is high but not fatal: no found paper claims (b) transferring the *search policy itself* across architectures/tokenizers, or (c) validating calibrated extrapolation uncertainty against an independently trained, genuinely held-out large run (all validate via in-pool cross-validation or same-family scale-up). That pairing is the narrowest surviving gap, but the space is moving fast enough (5 close papers in 13 months) that it may close before the thesis does.
