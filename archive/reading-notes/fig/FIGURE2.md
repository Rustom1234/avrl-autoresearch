## Captions

**Figure 2 (`fig2_in_training`).** Corrupted-reward groups admitted to training, out of 600 unverified groups at an equal 200-expensive-call budget per arm, one panel per setting (GSM8K at 1.5B and 0.5B policy size, and HelpSteer2 with a reward-model proxy for the open-ended task). Allocators are ordered threshold, greedy, learned within each panel, with the consistency-ranked threshold added last in the open-ended panel. Each dot is one training seed; the bar is the seed mean. Blue marks label-free rules (rank by the cheap grader's own confidence signal, no refitting); orange marks learned allocators (greedy self-trainer or the bandit-flavoured learned allocator, both refit on bought labels each step). Lower is better.

**Figure 2b (`fig2b_eval`).** Same panel layout and allocator ordering as Figure 2, plotted against the final held-out quality metric instead: eval accuracy (mean of 300 held-out prompts) for the two GSM8K panels, eval judge score (mean of 200 held-out prompts) for the HelpSteer2 panel, both read from each run's step-100 evaluation. Same color coding as Figure 2.

## What the figure shows

The label-free threshold admits the fewest corrupted-reward groups in every setting tested: vote agreement on GSM8K (both policy sizes) and response consistency on HelpSteer2 are each the lowest bar in their panel, including beating the weaker reward-model-gap threshold on HelpSteer2 (consistency mean 24 vs. RM-gap mean 37.7) and both learned allocators on GSM8K 0.5B (threshold mean 122.5 vs. greedy mean 129 and learned/bandit mean 172). The learned allocators lose to or at best tie the winning threshold in every panel (on HelpSteer2, greedy beats only the weak RM-gap threshold, 28 vs. 37.7, but still trails the consistency threshold's 24; on both GSM8K panels, greedy and learned/bandit both trail the threshold), and the bandit-flavoured learned allocator is the single worst arm in every panel where it runs (GSM8K 1.5B: 62.5 mean vs. 26 and 34; GSM8K 0.5B: 172 mean vs. 122.5 and 129; HelpSteer2: 38.7 mean, narrowly above even the weak RM-gap threshold's 37.7). Final held-out quality ties across allocators within each setting: GSM8K accuracy sits in a roughly 0.65-0.70 band at 1.5B and a roughly 0.39-0.44 band at 0.5B regardless of allocator, and HelpSteer2 judge scores all fall in a roughly 4.4-4.7 band, with the consistency threshold's slightly higher mean (4.62) not separated from the rest at two seeds.

## Caveat on the eval panels (added 2026-10-02 20:50 UTC)
Each seed's held-out subset is drawn with that seed, so compare markers of the same seed across arms (paired), not absolute levels across seeds. HumanEval (Figure 3 legend) is the fixed 164-problem set and is comparable across seeds.
